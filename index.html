About

Operator in HR, builder everywhere else

I do the consulting work because it pays and the building work because I can't stop. The two inform each other — half of what I build for clients started as something I needed in my own company first.

I work with small-business owners who need an HR partner but can't justify a full-time hire. My job is to take the part of running a company that nobody trained for — handbooks, comp structures, employee issues, compliance, the recruiting that eats a week — and turn it into a system someone else can run.

On the side, I co-founded CoaPro because the same pattern kept showing up with AI: people were using it whether their employer had a policy or not, and most companies were either banning it (which goes underground) or paying for seats nobody used well. The fix is coaching in the moment and a small data set, not a bigger surveillance stack.

I write code in Swift, Python, TypeScript, and whatever else a project needs. I trained four of CoaPro's models on my own GPU, including a small language model that scores prompts against a rubric. Most weekends I'm either outside or shipping something I started on a Tuesday night.
