About

Operator in HR, builder everywhere else

I work with small-business owners who need an HR partner but can't justify a full-time hire. My job is to take the part of running a company that nobody trained for — handbooks, comp, employee issues, compliance, recruiting — and turn it into a system someone else can run.

On the side I co-founded CoaPro because the same pattern kept showing up with AI: people use it whether their employer has a policy or not, and most companies either ban it (it goes underground) or pay for seats nobody uses well. Coaching in the moment and a small data set, not a bigger surveillance stack.

I write Swift, Python, TypeScript, and whatever a project needs. I trained four of CoaPro's models on my own GPU, including a small language model that scores prompts against a rubric.
